How myPod works

      

        
1
Get a free Soulseek account
          
myPod has its own Soulseek downloader built in, so you do not need to keep the Soulseek app. You only need an account, which is a username and password. The simplest way to make one is the official Soulseek app: open it once, choose a username and password, then type them into Settings. You only do this once.

          
Close the Soulseek app while myPod is downloading. An account can only be signed in in one place at a time.

          

        
2
Check your two folders
          
In Settings, under "Where your music is saved", you will see an iPod folder and a Music folder (explained below). The defaults are fine. Change them only if you want your music somewhere else.

        
3
Paste a Spotify link
          
On the Download page, paste one of these:

          
  • A playlist link, like open.spotify.com/playlist/...
    • 
          
  • A profile link, like open.spotify.com/user/.... myPod lists that person's public playlists so you can choose which ones.

          
In Spotify press Share, then Copy link. The playlist has to be public. For a very long playlist, Spotify only shares the first 100 songs.

        
4
Choose what you want
          
Pick MP3 (plays on any iPod) or FLAC (CD quality, big files, for your own collection). You can set it for every playlist, for each playlist, or even for each song. Tick the playlists you want, pick which iPod they are for, and press Download.

        
5
Put it on your iPod
          
Plug the iPod in. When the download finishes, a "Put it on your player" box appears. Pick the iPod and press Send. myPod connects the iPod only for the upload, copies the songs, and ejects it, so when it says "Ejected" it is safe to unplug.

      


      
Your two folders

      

        
iPod folder: MP3 only
Everything here is an MP3, because that is what an iPod plays. This is what gets copied onto your iPod.

        
Music folder: best quality
Your own collection, in the quality you chose, MP3 or FLAC. Turn off "Also keep a best-quality copy" on the Download page if you only want the iPod folder.

        
Same song in both?
If a song is an MP3 in both, it is stored once, not twice. A FLAC song keeps the FLAC in the Music folder and a normal MP3 copy in the iPod folder.

      


      
Good to know

      

        
A song says "not found"
Not every song is shared on Soulseek. myPod accepts a different version of a song (live, remaster) if the original is not there. Songs it cannot find are listed when it finishes.

        
"Already have it" / "already on" the iPod
myPod checks your folders, and the iPod you choose, before downloading, so it never downloads or stores the same song twice. Press Scan songs on the Player page so it knows what is on each iPod.

        
Which iPods work?
Tested on the iPod classic and the iPod nano (4th generation). iPods that show up as a drive on your Mac when plugged in should work. The iPod touch and iPhone do not.

        
FLAC needs a helper
Making an MP3 copy of a FLAC song uses a free tool called ffmpeg. If it is missing, myPod tells you.

        
Is myPod free?
Yes. If it saves you time there is a Support this app button in Settings.
